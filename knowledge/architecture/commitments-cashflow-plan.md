# Commitments and cash-flow delivery

Scope: one-off and monthly bills/receivables, due/overdue/completed/skipped states, explicit settlement against an existing movement or one new manual movement, and a per-account forecast from recorded balances plus outstanding commitments. Monthly day 29–31 clamps to the month's last day. A commitment never posts money just because its due date arrived. Existing movements remain the only source of actual income/expenses.

Occurrences are generated from schedules; completed/skipped occurrences retain snapshots. Editing a schedule updates pending occurrences only. Ending a series preserves past pending items and recorded history. Payment linking is explicit, checks ownership/account/direction and prohibits using a movement twice. Reversed or incompatible linked movements return to review/outstanding state. Settlement uses the existing transaction and command-receipt boundaries for atomicity and retries.

Forecast uses today's São Paulo date, includes overdue pending commitments with an explicit subtotal, and separates available money, benefits and reserves. It never adds the monthly budget again. Planned amounts are estimates, not bank balances. Dashboard shows the current month's forecast; Planning provides month-scoped commitment management and up to twelve months of projection. Privacy hiding covers forecast values as well.

Delivery: schema with workspace RLS and cascading deletion; integration ports; versioned API; responsive tabs, forms and completion review; portable backup/restore; domain/database and browser checks; apply migration before publishing the dependent code.

Implemented: Planning now has Orçamento mensal / Contas e previsão navigation. Commitment management includes account/search/status filters, create/edit/end, one-off/monthly recurrence, variable actual amounts, linking with a configurable date range, skip/reopen and idempotent retry. Dashboard has a separate forecast section that follows the existing privacy control; the analysis month continues to control realized financial reports independently. Existing monthly budgets remain estimates, without automatic copying from recurring obligations.

Operational bounds: 200 schedule definitions per workspace, up to twelve months forward, first 1,000 due items visible with total coverage disclosed, candidate search up to 366 days/100 entries. Forecast includes all outstanding occurrences up to the horizon, including prior overdue amounts, independent of the visible list cap. Ending a series cancels pending dates from today onward and preserves earlier debts and recorded snapshots. Original account/type/start/frequency identities are immutable; changing those requires ending the old series and creating a new one. Monthly dates clamp without permanently drifting after February. No browser notification or bank-payment automation is claimed.

Schema: two workspace-owned tables with forced RLS, composite owner/workspace foreign keys and cascading space deletion. No cron is needed to create recurring rows: due dates/statuses are derived, while concluded/dispensed/review snapshots are persisted. Settlement takes the account lock before the workspace commitment lock; the existing command receipt and database transaction cover both financial creation and completion. Backup v8 includes these definitions and snapshots; CSV continues to contain only real ledger movements.
